Key Definitions:
EAL = English as an Additional Language (learning English while living in an English-speaking environment)
EFL = English as a Foreign Language (learning English in a non-English-speaking country)
CEFR = Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (A1-C2 proficiency scale)
IGCSE = International General Certificate of Secondary Education
Language levels progress from A1 (beginner) to C2 (proficient)
